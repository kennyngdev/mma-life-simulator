## Immediate blockers

Any one of these blocks release regardless of the retelling total:

- An impossible or untruthful matchup explanation.
- An essential action hidden or obstructed at 320 px width.
- A lost or corrupted active save.
- One biography overwriting another, including a same-seed replay.

For each defect, record the session, device/viewport, locale, career ID, seed, phase, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot or exported biography when available.
